GRaCe-GRC is an open-source Governance, Risk and Compliance platform. It reads regulatory
documents, proposes the controls they require with AI assistance, and sends every proposal to
expert review. Approved controls are published as machine-readable catalogs, then monitored
continuously with automated audit evidence, and finally scored for risk and compliance in reports.

## Projects

| | Project | What it does | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Federated Regulatory Control Mapping System](https://github.com/GRaCe-GRC/GRaCe-GRC-docs/blob/main/systems/control-mapping/README.md) | Turns regulatory files into reviewed controls, control mappings and catalogs | Months 0–12 |
| 2 | [Continuous Compliance Monitoring and Automated Audit Evidence System](https://github.com/GRaCe-GRC/GRaCe-GRC-docs/blob/main/systems/continuous-compliance/README.md) | Tests controls continuously and collects audit evidence automatically | Months 9–27 |
| 3 | [Risk Assessment Compliance Scoring and Reporting Platform](https://github.com/GRaCe-GRC/GRaCe-GRC-docs/blob/main/systems/risk-reporting/README.md) | Scores risk and compliance, tracks remediation and produces reports | Months 24–36 |

For more information, visit the [documentation hub](https://github.com/GRaCe-GRC/GRaCe-GRC-docs).
